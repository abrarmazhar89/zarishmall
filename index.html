Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2025

At Zarish Mall, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through our WhatsApp checkout, we collect your Full Name, Phone Number, and Delivery Address. This information is provided voluntarily by you and is required solely to process and deliver your order.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used exclusively for order processing and delivery coordination. We may contact you via WhatsApp or phone to confirm your order or provide delivery updates.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to any third parties, advertisers, or external organizations. Your data is used only for fulfilling your Zarish Mall orders.

4. Payment & Financial Data

All orders are processed via Cash on Delivery (COD) through WhatsApp. We do not collect, store, or process any payment card information, bank account details, or digital wallet data.

5. Data Security

Order information exchanged via WhatsApp is protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption. We take reasonable precautions to ensure your information remains secure.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy concerns or requests, please contact us directly on WhatsApp: +92 322 4061289
